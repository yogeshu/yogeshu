## About me

I'm a frontend engineer based in India, currently working at **MilliPixels Interactive LLP**. I specialize in building production-ready interfaces and frontend systems with **React, Next.js, TypeScript, and modern JavaScript**.

I care about the details that make a product feel trustworthy: thoughtful UX, maintainable architecture, responsive design, accessibility, performance, and a clean developer experience. I also enjoy turning lessons from real projects into useful tools, writing, and mentorship.

Outside engineering, I'm the author of **[Love, Loss, and Life](https://www.amazon.in/Love-Loss-Life-Yogesh-Bhavsar-ebook/dp/B0DX2JYDK8/)** and the creator of **[Makhanchor](https://makhanchor.in)**, a home for poetry and creative work.
